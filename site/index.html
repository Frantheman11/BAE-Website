Qualifying smart money

Smart money is investment from businesses whose interests line up with the company raising it. Before any introduction, we qualify every investor.

I

Strategic fit

Your business gains when the company wins: as a customer, a supplier or a partner in its market.

II

Industry knowledge

You know the market the company sells into, and the people in it.

III

More than capital

You bring a channel, customers or expertise, not only a check.

IV

Qualified to invest

Your investor status is confirmed before any offering documents are shared.

V

Track record

References, reputation and past investments are reviewed.

VI

Aligned for the long term

You want the company to win, and you are patient enough to help it get there.
